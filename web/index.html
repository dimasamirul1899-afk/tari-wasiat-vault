Wasiat

A digital inheritance vault on Tari Ootle. Lock XTM for someone you love, prove you are still here with a heartbeat — and if you ever go silent past the deadline, only they can claim it.

DEMO SIMULATION — no wallet, no real funds. Every action shows the Ootle call it maps to, and refusals use the template's exact messages.

1 · Act as

The template checks who is calling. Pick a role — the demo enforces the same rules on-chain.

You are acting as: owner. The owner creates the vault, sends heartbeats, and can cancel. Only the beneficiary can claim — and only after the deadline.

2 · Chain epoch (simulated)

Current epoch: 1000  ·  the deadline is an epoch, not a date — a mainnet epoch is about 20 minutes, so 100 epochs ≈ 33 hours. A real vault would use a much longer silence period.

3 · Create the vault

Maps to WasiatVault::create(bucket, beneficiary, beneficiary_account, inactivity_epochs, owner_account). The owner is whoever creates the vault.
